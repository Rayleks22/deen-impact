The minimum admission age is 2 years for our Pre-Nursery early childhood programme. We also admit pupils and students across Nursery 1 & 2, Primary 1 through 5, and College (Junior and Senior Secondary, JSS 1 to SSS 3).
Every prospective pupil or student undertakes a diagnostic entrance assessment tailored to their age group. For early years, this involves gentle observational and cognitive tasks. For Primary and College applicants, it covers literacy, numeracy, and general reasoning. An interview with both the child and parents follows to assess learning habits, moral orientation, and placement readiness.
No. DeenImpact Academy is strictly a day school. There are no boarding facilities. Our day school model allows parents to maintain active involvement in their children's spiritual and moral development each evening following our structured daily school routine.
We operate a blended curriculum. This harmonises high-standard Nigerian national curriculum benchmarks with an advanced emphasis on science, technology, and authentic Islamic religious practice following the Sunnah (including Tahfeez and Arabic). We systematically prepare our students for all major external exams (Common Entrance, BECE, WAEC, NECO, and JAMB).
Our class size is strictly capped at 15 students to 1 teacher. This small-class model ensures that our teachers provide personalized academic attention, identify areas requiring improvement promptly, and nurture strong teacher-student mentorship.
Admissions are always open throughout the academic year. Parents can apply via:
  • Walk-in: Visit our campus at Olomo Quarters 2, Zone 2, near Olose Junction, Apete, Ibadan.
  • Phone Call: Call our administrative line on 08123458536.
  • WhatsApp: Message us at https://wa.me/2348123458536.
  • Download Form: Download our printable Admission Form PDF from the website.
Result sheets are managed securely through our dedicated Student Results Portal. Parents enter their child's unique Admission Number and secure PIN to download the official PDF report card. Physical printed report sheets are also available for pickup at the school office.
Our administrative office is open from Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on weekends and declared public holidays.
