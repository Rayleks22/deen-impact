Academics & Curriculum

Blended curriculum, small classes of 15 students to 1 teacher, comprehensive exam prep, Qur'an memorisation, and practical vocational trades.

Our Educational Model

The Blended Curriculum Philosophy

At DeenImpact Academy, academic rigour is seamlessly integrated with Islamic religious practice following the Sunnah, technical innovation, and practical skills.

📚

15:1 Student-Teacher Ratio

Every class is capped at 15 students per teacher. This intimate learning environment guarantees that instructors observe each child's learning pace, rectify gaps immediately, and foster deep participation.

🔬

Science & Tech Focus

STEM learning is embedded across all levels. Practical physics, chemistry, biology in our science lab and hands-on coding and digital literacy in our computer lab nurture analytical problem-solvers.

🕌

Sunnah & Islamic Studies

Islamic Studies (IRS), Arabic language, Hadith, and Adab (etiquette) are taught following the authentic Sunnah. Students pray daily congregational Zuhr and Asr prayers in our campus mosque.

Class Breakdown

Educational Levels Offered

Progressive stages tailored to developmental milestones from age 2 through graduation.

AGES 2 – 5 YEARS

Nursery & Early Years

Pre-Nursery • Nursery 1 • Nursery 2

Focuses on early sensory motor skills, Montessori-informed active learning, phonics, number concepts, handwriting, basic Arabic letters, and short Qur'an Surahs. A safe, warm environment where children discover a love for learning.

PRIMARY EDUCATION

Primary School

Primary 1 to Primary 5

Core subjects include English Language, Mathematics, Basic Science & Technology, Social Studies, Agricultural Science, Computer Studies, Arabic Language, Islamic Studies (IRS), and Tahfeez. Prepares pupils thoroughly for national Common Entrance.

SECONDARY COLLEGE

College (Secondary)

JSS 1–3 (Junior) • SSS 1–3 (Senior)

Advanced academic pathways across Sciences, Arts, and Commercial disciplines. Students engage in practical laboratory sessions, vocational trade mastery, and intensive coaching for BECE, WAEC, NECO, and JAMB.

Academic Milestone Assessments

Major External Examinations Prepared For

Our curriculum and mock testing cycles systematically prepare pupils and students for all major Nigerian external examinations.

Primary & Junior College Exams

  • National Common Entrance Examination (NCEE): Rigorous coaching in quantitative, verbal, mathematics, and English for admission into leading colleges.
  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE): Comprehensive preparation across core subjects for Junior College transition to Senior College.

Senior College & Tertiary Exams

  • WAEC (WASSCE) & NECO (SSCE): Intense theoretical review, laboratory practicals, past-question drills, and continuous mock assessments.
  • JAMB (UTME): Computer-based test (CBT) training in our computer lab to ensure speed, accuracy, and high scores for university admissions.
Distinctive Enrichment

Special Programmes

Unique academic and life skills programmes exclusive to DeenImpact Academy students.

Spiritual & Moral Excellence

Qur'an Memorisation (Tahfeez)

Conducted with certified instructors, our Tahfeez programme allows students to memorize portions of the Holy Qur'an with proper Tajweed (rules of recitation), memorisation tracking, and understanding. Emphasizes daily revision (Muraja'ah) and applying the Sunnah in daily interaction.

Practical Self-Reliance

Vocational Trades Programme

In addition to intellectual pursuits, every student is introduced to practical vocational trades. Students learn hands-on crafts, technical design, domestic economics, and entrepreneurship skills. This cultivates self-reliance, work ethic, and creative capability for life beyond school.

Hands-on Application

Laboratories & Co-Curricular Clubs

Fostering teamwork, communication, and applied science outside regular lectures.

💻

Computer Laboratory

Dedicated workstations with high-speed access. Students learn keyboarding, office software, internet safety, coding fundamentals, and computer-based test (CBT) practice.

🧪

Science Laboratory

Equipped for physics, chemistry, and biology demonstrations and student experiments. Safety equipment and apparatus allow students to see classroom theory become physical reality.

⚙️

JETS Club

Junior Engineers, Technicians, and Scientists club. Members design simple circuits, build science models, compete in science quizzes, and solve local challenges through STEM.

📰

Press Club

Develops articulate writers, interviewers, and public speakers. Students produce the school notice board bulletins, coordinate assembly presentations, and practice ethical journalism.

🤝

Rotaract Club

Fosters leadership, community service, social responsibility, and teamwork. Students organize environmental cleanups, empathy drives, and peer mentorship.

🕌

Tahfeez & Islamic Circle

Daily halaqahs, Tajweed circles, and moral discussions following the Sunnah, helping students develop exemplary character, humility, and leadership.
