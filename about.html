About DeenImpact Academy

Rooted in our motto: Knowledge, Action and Guidance. Blending science, technology and authentic Islamic practice in Apete, Ibadan.

Established 2017

Our Heritage & Spiritual Foundation

DeenImpact Academy was founded in 2017 in Apete, Ibadan as a private day institution serving families across Nursery, Primary, and College (Senior Secondary) levels.

The school’s foundational ethos is built upon the synthesis of two vital pillars: uncompromising modern academic inquiry in science and technology, alongside sincere Islamic religious practice, following the Sunnah.

We believe that knowledge without action is incomplete, and action without divine guidance lacks direction. Through this tripartite motto—Knowledge, Action and Guidance—we prepare our pupils and students to thrive academically, morally, and socially.

What Sets DeenImpact Apart

  • ✓ Integrated Science & Islamic Ethos: Practical science education hand-in-hand with daily Sunnah application and moral discipline.
  • ✓ Small Class Size (15:1): Strictly maintained ratio of 15 students to 1 teacher ensures every child's academic and emotional needs are addressed.
  • ✓ Qur'an Memorisation (Tahfeez): Structured Tajweed recitation and memorisation woven into the academic routine.
  • ✓ Practical Vocational Trades: Technical and practical craftsmanship training for life skills and entrepreneurship.
  • ✓ Secure Learning Campus: Gated perimeter, trained security guards, clinic sick bay, first-aid readiness, and reliable utilities.
Historical Journey

The Story of DeenImpact Academy

From its establishment in 2017, DeenImpact Academy began with a steadfast vision to address a critical need in Ibadan: providing children with premier academic instruction in science, arts, and mathematics without compromising on pristine Islamic values rooted in the Quran and the authentic Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

Starting with early years and nursery learning, the Academy progressively expanded into a full-fledged Primary School and College, establishing modern science laboratories, computer training labs, a dedicated school mosque, and interactive classrooms.

Direction & Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our guiding light shaping classroom instruction, student conduct, and community service.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a leading center of educational excellence that nurtures intellectually vibrant, scientifically innovative, and morally upright Muslim youth who positively impact their communities and the world at large.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a balanced, high-standard curriculum integrating science, technology, vocational skills, and Qur'anic values within a small-class, student-centered environment that fosters critical thinking and authentic Sunnah discipline.

⭐

Our Core Values

  • Taqwa: God-consciousness and sincerity in all endeavors.
  • Academic Rigour: Relentless pursuit of intellectual mastery.
  • Integrity (Amanah): Honesty, transparency, and accountability.
  • Discipline: Punctuality, order, and respect for all.
  • Innovation: Creative problem-solving in science and technology.
