Admissions Are Always Open

We welcome new pupils and transfer students throughout the academic session across all levels: Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1–2, Primary 1–5, JSS 1–3, and SSS 1–3. The minimum admission age for our early learning program is 2 years.

Step-by-Step Guide

Our 4-Step Admission Process

Follow these four straightforward steps to register your child at DeenImpact Academy.

1

1. Enquire & Obtain Form

Visit our campus in Apete, Ibadan, call us on 08123458536, or send a WhatsApp message. You can also download the printable Admission Form directly from our website.

2

2. Entrance Exam & Interview

Prospective students undergo an age-appropriate entrance assessment to evaluate baseline literacy, numeracy, and reasoning skills, accompanied by a friendly student-parent interaction.

3

3. Document Submission

Return the completed admission form with required credentials: child's birth certificate, recent passport photographs, and previous school academic report records (for transfer students).

4

4. Admission Offer & Resumption

Successful applicants receive an official Offer of Admission letter, school handbook, uniform guide, and resumption schedule. Your child is officially enrolled into the DeenImpact family!

Required Documents

What to Bring Along

When attending your child's entrance assessment and interview, please ensure you have the following items ready:

  • ✓ Completed Admission Form: Filled and signed by parent or legal guardian.
  • ✓ Birth Certificate / Age Declaration: Verifying the child is at least 2 years old.
  • ✓ Recent Passport Photographs: 2 clear passport photos with white background.
  • ✓ Previous Academic Report Card: Last cumulative term results from previous school (if transferring).
