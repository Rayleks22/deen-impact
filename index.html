About DeenImpact Academy

Where Academic Excellence Meets Spiritual Integrity

Academic Progression

Levels Offered: Pre-Nursery to SS3

Continuous, coherent learning pathways designed to guide your child at every stage of their intellectual and emotional development.

Nursery & Early Years

PRE-NURSERY • NURSERY 1 & 2

Gentle, stimulating early childhood learning from age 2. Developing phonics, early numeracy, social etiquette, motor skills, and foundational Islamic habits in a nurturing setting.

Primary School

PRIMARY 1 TO PRIMARY 5

Robust foundational education emphasizing English comprehension, mathematics, science, digital skills, Qur'an recitation, and preparation for national Common Entrance examinations.

College (Secondary)

JSS 1 TO SSS 3

Comprehensive secondary education covering sciences, arts, and commercial subjects. Targeted preparation for BECE, WAEC, NECO, and JAMB, alongside vocational trade training.
